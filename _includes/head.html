Teachers print these lessons for students, so the printed page is a real target, and the old stylesheet's print intent carries over: black text on plain paper. The Design System's blue and off-white bands lose their background and their white and gold text turns black — browsers drop background colours when printing, which would otherwise leave a white title on a white page — and the deep bands shrink so the title is not alone at the top of the sheet. The pagination rules keep a heading with what follows it and an image or list item on one page; .row is deliberately not in that list, because a lesson's whole body is one .row, and a block taller than a page that must not break gets pushed to a fresh page first, leaving a blank one behind.

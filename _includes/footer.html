DSPIRA

Digital Signal Processing in Radio Astronomy

Free lessons on building and using a horn radio telescope, written by the high school teachers who did it. Produced by WVU RAIL with the Green Bank Observatory, funded by the National Science Foundation.

Lessons are free to use.

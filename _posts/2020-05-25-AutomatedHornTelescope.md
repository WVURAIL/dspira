A twenty-three step Instructables build of an automated radio telescope. On the
antenna, the author is direct about where it came from:

> So, the first part of the project was making an antenna to improve the
> signal. I could have used a typical satellite dish, and it would probably
> work, but I used the design from DSPIRA.

What the DSPIRA lessons do not cover is pointing the thing by machine. This
build adds a motorised mount and drives it, which is the obvious next step
after a few nights of turning a horn by hand and writing down the angle.

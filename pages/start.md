## Try an activity {#try}

**No telescope needed:** [How Fast Are We Moving?]({{ '/HowFastAreWeMoving' | relative_url }}) explores orbital motion.
Choose the basic, advanced, or physics worksheet. You need internet access and the activity materials.

For a software activity, [the introductory GNU Radio lab]({{ '/dsplab-intro/' | relative_url }}) explores signals and sound.
Install GNU Radio before starting. Later receiver labs require additional equipment.

[Teaching notes and preparation]({{ '/teach/' | relative_url }}) help you choose material for a class.

## Build a telescope {#build}

A complete telescope needs an antenna, stand, amplifier, receiver, cables, and a suitable computer.
Budget for several hundred dollars in parts. Prices vary; confirm current costs before ordering.
The [starter kit]({{ '/kits/' | relative_url }}) supplies only the receiver and amplifier, subject to availability.

1. Review [the complete parts overview]({{ '/BuildingHornTelescope_Overview' | relative_url }}).
2. Build the antenna using [Horn Construction]({{ '/categories/horn-construction/' | relative_url }}).
3. Choose the amplifier and receiver in [Receiver Electronics]({{ '/categories/receiver-electronics/' | relative_url }}).
4. Check [software compatibility]({{ '/software/' | relative_url }}) before preparing your computer.
5. Continue to [Observing]({{ '/categories/observing/' | relative_url }}) when the system is ready.

Construction needs tools, workspace, and appropriate supervision. Review the instructions before scheduling class sessions.
[Other horn designs]({{ '/Other_Horn_Designs' | relative_url }}) are optional alternatives, not additional required builds.

## Use your telescope {#observe}

Start with [telescope setup]({{ '/Telescope_Setup' | relative_url }}), then [run the spectrometer]({{ '/HornOperation_runningSpectrometer' | relative_url }}).
Follow [the calibration procedure]({{ '/HornOperation_Calibration' | relative_url }}) before collecting calibrated observations.
The [Observing module]({{ '/categories/observing/' | relative_url }}) continues through saving and analyzing data.

[LightWork technical guides](/lightwork/) provide additional construction and measurement references.

The lessons are free and nobody has to tell us they are using them, so this is
not a complete list — it is what we know about. If your group belongs on it,
[say so in the forum]({{ '/forum/' | relative_url }}) or email
[{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

Groups that built the hardware and published what they found have their work in
the [Community Labs]({{ '/categories/community-labs/' | relative_url }}) module
rather than here.

## Taught with

**[ASTR 735: Radio Astronomy](https://home.ifa.hawaii.edu/users/jpw/classes/radio/)**
— Institute for Astronomy, University of Hawai&#699;i. Jonathan Williams runs a
one-credit graduate and senior-undergraduate seminar whose summary is "a github
repo with everything you need to build a horn telescope", with DSPIRA among the
resources. Fall 2021.

**[For Teachers](https://greenbankobservatory.org/education/teachers/)** —
Green Bank Observatory. DSPIRA is described there as the six-week summer
research programme the observatory ran with WVU for ten high school teachers a
year.

## Presented

**[Interferometry Using Very Affordable Radio Horn Telescopes](https://indico.phys.vt.edu/event/55/contributions/1314/attachments/1030/1441/CSAAPT_Presentation_F2023_Makous.pdf)**
— John Makous, Concord University. Sixteen slides on the two-horn
interferometer, from the basic theory through the Green Bank set-up to the
fringe results, given at the
[CSAAPT Fall 2023 meeting](https://indico.phys.vt.edu/event/55/timetable/?print=1&view=standard).
Makous also wrote LightWork Memo 31 and contributed to
[gr-radio_astro](https://github.com/WVURAIL/gr-radio_astro).

**[Observing Galactic Neutral Hydrogen Using Radioastronomy](https://meetings-archive.aps.org/eglss/2023/k01/4)**
— poster, 2023 Spring Meeting of the APS Eastern Great Lakes Section. A
portable telescope of the DSPIRA design, built by a former student, used to
measure 21&nbsp;cm column densities and check them against published values.

**[Testing the Effectiveness of the DSPIRA Radio Telescope for Public Use](https://undergraduateresearch.wvu.edu/symposia/summer-2021/-poster-presentations/physical-sciences-poster-presentations-99-117/past-symposia/testing-the-effectiveness-of-the-dspira-radio-telescope-for-public-use)**
— Victoria Blanton, WVU Summer Undergraduate Research Experience 2021. The
[write-up is in Community Labs]({{ site.baseurl }}/TestingForPublicUse).

## Elsewhere

[their work is in Community Labs]({{ site.baseurl }}/WISRDUpdate).
